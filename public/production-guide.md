# STEVE — THE LAST BUILDER
## The Hero Who Rebuilt a Broken World

An original fan-made fictional chronicle.

## Character chronicle
STEVE wakes with no memory among the ruins of a fallen civilization. He is the last person who can channel the Worldheart, an ancient source of creative energy. Quiet, patient and brave, he listens before he acts. His greatest fear is failing the people who depend on him. His goal is to give everyone a safe home.

His obsidian armor has diamond edges and a glowing cyan square rune. His brown hair, block beard and cyan eyes remain visible. A black cape carries luminous square symbols. His sword cuts through corrupted stone; his true gift is shaping broken blocks into living places. The Worldheart cannot create from nothing: STEVE must gather materials, protect survivors and teach others to build.

## English voice-over — approximately 110–120 seconds
Read at about 135 words per minute. Bracketed directions are silent. Allow brief breaths at paragraph breaks. Dates are spoken in full.

[Quiet. A low pulse.]

When the world fell silent, one builder opened his eyes.
His name was STEVE.
He had no map. No home. No memory of the fall.
But his hands still knew how to build.

April twelfth, twenty twenty-five. The Awakening.
STEVE woke in a broken forest.
Beyond the trees, empty villages waited in the mist.
He chose to stay. He chose to help.

April thirteenth, twenty twenty-five. The First Night.
Monsters came out of the dark.
STEVE raised a shelter, block by block.
By sunrise, the last survivors had a place to rest.

April twentieth, twenty twenty-five. The Forgotten Power.
Deep in an ancient cave, he found the Worldheart.
Its blue light revealed a lost gift.
He could bring broken stone together again.
But the heart needed its missing key.

May second, twenty twenty-five. Into the Nether.
STEVE crossed a gate of fire.
Through ash and danger, he found the Ember Key.
His armor burned. His hope held strong.

May eighteenth, twenty twenty-five. The Final Dimension.
Above the dark towers, the Ender Dragon waited.
STEVE raised his sword.
One final strike broke the shadow's hold.
The power to rebuild was free.

[Music opens. Slow down.]

May nineteenth, twenty twenty-five. The Last Builder.
STEVE restored the Worldheart.
Forests grew. Rivers flowed. Families came home.
Then he taught them what his hands remembered.

He was the last builder of the old world.
He would not be the last of the new one.

A world is never lost while someone is willing to build.
